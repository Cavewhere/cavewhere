CaveWhere style prototypes

Four directions for a custom Qt Quick Controls style, each in light and dark. Every control on this page is live: hover, press, tab to it, type in it. Text is set in the fonts CaveWhere bundles today, switchable among the three families the Appearance settings offer. Companion document: CUSTOM_STYLE_PLAN.html covers how the chosen direction becomes a real style.

Decisions to settle before building the style

1. Direction

Chosen so far: D, built from your feedback. It keeps A's colors and adds B's 6 px corners with C's hairline borders and soft shadows. Three details in D are my calls and are open to change: dark input wells in dark mode where A used white blocks, a dark menu where A used a white one, and a spring-colored switch and slider.

2. Accent text in light mode

Spring #38BDD9 on white has a contrast ratio near 2.2, too faint for text. Each light theme here uses a deeper spring for editable values and keeps #38BDD9 for fills. Confirm the deeper shade reads as the same color to you.

3. Progress colors in light mode

Mint, cyan, and a white leading edge wash out on a light track. In light mode these prototypes run the sweep one step deeper, Teal through Blue, and lead with Navy.

4. Editable-value convention

The prototype colors every click-to-edit value with the accent. Today the app does the same through its link color. Confirm that links and editable values keep sharing one color.

5. Sidebar and chrome

Today's sidebar is a dark gradient in both modes. A, C, and D follow the mode; B keeps navy chrome in light mode. Choose whether chrome follows the mode or stays dark.

6. Busy indicator lifecycle

The droplet open and the expanding close add about half a second each. Choose whether short tasks skip them and show the spinning ring alone.
