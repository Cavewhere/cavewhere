What the right-hand pictures assume

  • T07ad as specified: each leg's tube, with its interpolated section, runs to the bisector plane; the corner envelope is their union; every elbow ring reaches from its own center to that envelope. At a 90° corner with unequal inside readings this leaves a 45° chamfer on the inside (solid green at f1: from the lead-in floor at 9 m to the shaft wall at −2 m; at f2: from the shaft's far wall up to the station). The dashed green line is the alternative of running each tube on until it leaves the other, which gives a square inside corner — a flat floor under the shaft at f2. Decided Oct 15: the dashed line, at both corners (the exit tube carried back under the shaft keeps its −8° dip, so that floor rises 0.28 m to the far wall). T07ad's spec now says so.
  • Bend-plane frames at a plumb corner: every elbow ring's frame lies in the plane of the two legs (right = the bend axis), and the exit leg starts on its horizontal frame at once; the transported roll is spread along the shaft, where the rings are round and it changes nothing. This is now T07af, run right after T07ad.
