# Passage tube rendering — known and suspected issues

Started Oct 20, 2026, after the builder rewrite landed (T07ak–T07an,
`e838a9f3`..`5db8e15c`). One entry per issue: what is seen or suspected,
where it comes from, how sure we are, and what would settle or fix it.
An issue that becomes a task gets a `T07..` id in `TUBE_RENDERING_FOLLOWUPS.md`
and its row here points at it; a fixed issue moves to the bottom.

States: **known** (seen in a test, a grab or the gallery), **suspected**
(reasoned from the code or the rules, unconfirmed), **decision** (needs a
ruling before anyone codes), **fixed** (commit named).

| I02 | known | `cwPassageTubeMeshCheck::wallReaches` | `WallMissed` false positives: the rule anchors a reading at its station, but T07am's end rings sit at the padded center `a − e·pad·u`, so a padded ring's walls are measured from the wrong point. | I04 | known | bends 150°–170° (cases 23, 31) | The hairpin pinch: the across-leg width is `(L+R)·cos(θ/2)`, so past ~150° the tube collapses toward `kMinRadiusMeters` and the sample rings cross (`SelfIntersection` 7–81, `Crease`).
