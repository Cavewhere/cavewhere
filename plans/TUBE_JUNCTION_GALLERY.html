Every rule works on ring columns only (vertex k of each ring along the arm), never on readings, so splay rings and scrap-outline rings go through the same code. Clean merge: (1) every through ring grows, per column, to the union of the two tubes: as far as the ray from the ring's center stays inside the arm's tube (its own rings, the last one carried on past J) clipped to the through's width (the through rings extruded across the arm, so a floor below the through floor still reaches the far wall's position). This carries the arm's section from the near wall to the far wall and tapers with the arm's own outline; rings outside the arm's footprint are untouched. (2) Trim every arm column where it first enters the grown through tube: the trimmed ring is the mouth and lies on the through surface. (3) Cut the through tube's quads inside the mouth loop (in its ring-index × column-index space) and zip the mouth loop to the boundary of the cut window. One closed manifold surface: no faces inside either tube, so transparency and fly-throughs work. The zip patch is not visible in a 2D cut; the cuts show the union outline. Overlap variants leave the arm's end inside the through tube and rely on the depth test; kept for comparison. Overlap variants and T07c test against J's section extruded along the through tangent; the clean merge tests against the through rings themselves, as the builder would.
